Real life check

Why keep a rulebook at all

A rulebook is not extra paperwork. It is the one page you can point to later and say "this is how I work."

It travels with you This same page continues into MC3, where you'll deepen it. Nothing here is thrown away.
It proves you can explain it Writing your habits in your own words, and explaining them to a peer, shows real understanding.
It keeps you consistent The same routine, every time, on every real task — not something you reinvent each time.
That's why this section is a mandatory, gated step — the rulebook page must be completed, not skipped.
