Why it matters

Why keep a rulebook?

It is not extra paperwork. It is one page you can point to and say: this is how I work.

It travels with you

You will add to this same page in MC3. Nothing here is thrown away.

It shows you understand

Writing your habits in your own words, and explaining them to a peer, shows real understanding.

It keeps you consistent

The same routine, every time, on every real task.

That is why this is a required step. You must complete your rulebook page. It cannot be skipped.
